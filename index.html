📄 Import a versioned monthly plan

Select the month first. The app accepts exactly one immutable Soll plan as that month's baseline. Every later upload becomes Revision 1, Revision 2, and so on—automatically and without a manual Soll/revision switch.

For a PDF, its creation timestamp is used automatically when available. Completed shifts before this boundary are frozen.
1 · Immutable historyCompleted or already tracked shifts are never rewritten.
2 · Future-only revisionOnly future records inside the document's stated coverage period may change.
3 · Complete origin labelEvery active row says Soll plan, Revision 1, Revision 2, or needs review.
4 · Duplicate guardIdentical files and identical normalized plan contents are rejected before a version is created.
